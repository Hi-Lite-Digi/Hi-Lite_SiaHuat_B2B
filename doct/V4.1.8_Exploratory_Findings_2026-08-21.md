# V4.1.8 Exploratory QA and Fix Report — 21 August 2026

## Scope

- Local verification target: `http://localhost:3002`
- Production URL for post-deployment verification: https://hi-lite-sia-huat-b2-b.vercel.app/
- Test style: formal regression plus novel, messy and multi-turn customer conversations
- Catalogue authority: Supabase product catalogue, with live Sia Huat stock checks at the checkout gate

## Defects reproduced and fixed

1. Natural quantities such as “two dozen” were not consistently converted to numeric stock requirements.
2. Product subtypes drifted: serving spoons could include tongs, wok lids could include complete woks, and sharpeners could include knives.
3. A correction in the same message could retain the old colour or quantity.
4. Typo-heavy requests such as “noodal strainner” could return an adjacent but wrong product.
5. A recommendation request could ask another question instead of selecting from the latest visible options.
6. English follow-ups after Chinese messages could remain in Chinese.
7. Image recognition completed correctly in n8n, but the old two-stage AI path exceeded the app timeout and showed a misleading fallback.
8. Image results could mix a different construction/family, such as a stainless counter dispenser among plastic insulated carriers.

## Fixes applied

- Added word, dozen and Chinese quantity parsing, with latest-correction precedence.
- Added strict subtype/category validation for serving spoons, wok lids, knife sharpeners, knives and food strainers.
- Added latest-colour and product-switch handling.
- Added typo normalization and stronger conversation-memory routing.
- Added a deterministic recommendation path using the newest displayed products.
- Added image-narrative grounding against Supabase product codes.
- Added image family/material consistency filtering.
- Changed the n8n Sales Assistant model from `gpt-5-mini` to `gpt-4.1-mini` for faster grounded image replies.
- Kept the total image request timeout below the 30-second customer target.
